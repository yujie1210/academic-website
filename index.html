Yujie Fan (Nichole)

Yujie Fan (Nichole) is a PhD candidate in Economics at Nanyang Technological University, supervised by Associate Professor Wang Wenjie and co-supervised by Professor Yohanes Eko Riyanto. Her research lies at the intersection of experimental economics, human-AI interaction, and applied machine learning.

Prior to joining NTU, she received an MPhil in Economics from the University of Cambridge and a BSc (Hons) in Economics with First Class Distinction from the University of Nottingham Ningbo China.
