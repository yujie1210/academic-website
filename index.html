Yujie Fan (Nichole)

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Nanyang Technological University, supervised by Associate Professor Wang Wenjie and co-supervised by Professor Yohanes Eko Riyanto. My research interests sit at the intersection of experimental economics, machine learning applications, and human-AI interaction, with ongoing work on matching in two-sided markets and human-AI interaction in teams. I am also actively involved in undergraduate teaching in economics and econometrics.
